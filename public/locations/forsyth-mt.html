    Sell Mineral Rights in Forsyth, MT

    Forsyth is the Rosebud County seat on the Yellowstone River. A retired engineer explains the courthouse records and how to evaluate a CBM-era interest here.

    Forsyth is where every real title check on a Rosebud County mineral interest has to start, and the courthouse there tells a more complete story than most people expect.

    Sitting on the Yellowstone River, Forsyth is the Rosebud County seat and home to the Clerk and Recorder's office that holds title records for everything from the Colstrip coal country to the quieter reaches near Ashland and Birney. If you're trying to figure out what you actually own anywhere in this county, this is the starting point.

    What a Forsyth Title Search Actually Shows

    A courthouse search here will show your recorded chain of title, any current oil and gas leases, and whether coal was reserved separately at some point in your family's ownership history, which is common in this county given its mining background. We've seen owners surprised to learn their deed splits coal from oil and gas entirely, sometimes to different original grantees, decades before anyone in the family remembers.

    Rosebud County's Coalbed Methane Era

    The late 1990s and 2000s CBM boom put wells across parts of Rosebud County, concentrated more heavily toward Colstrip than directly around Forsyth itself, though the county as a whole has more oil and gas production history than the quieter reservation-interior towns to its south. Most of that production is mature today, in steady decline rather than fresh development.

    What we'd Check for a Forsyth-Area Tract

    We want your recorded deed, any current division order, and confirmation of whether coal was severed separately. From there we evaluate the underlying oil and gas interest on its own merits, current production if any, decline history, and proximity to the more active Colstrip-area CBM belt.
